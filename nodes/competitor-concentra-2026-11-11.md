## Observed on the page

- The /employers/ address redirected to the site homepage, so the homepage is what was measured. The sub-page structure noted in July (#15) was not measured.
- H1: "Workforce Health That Works." The page highlights Concentra HUB (online authorizations, test results and reporting for employers, workers' compensation payors and TPAs).
- Page text states "12,000+ colleagues", "more than 50,000 patients a day", "630+ occupational health centers" and "420+ onsite health clinics at employer locations". The sentence also begins with a year fragment that was cut by the page layout, so the stat date is not confirmed.
- Service links include DOT physicals, employee drug testing, telemedicine, onsite clinics, travel health and vaccinations, a drug-free workplace item (CMCA) and a medical review item.
- Calls to action: "Get started now", "Schedule a telemedicine visit", "Contact Us", "Cures Act Request". No amounts shown. 1 form input (search).

## July 2 claims against the live page

- July 2 node: hero "Keeping America's workforce healthy"; 520 centers; 11,000 colleagues; 40+ years. The live homepage shows a different H1 and larger figures (630+ centers, 12,000+ colleagues). "40+ years" was not matched.

## Caveats

- Homepage only. 155 of the 441 words are in header, nav or footer, which is the largest chrome share among the 11 pages except Latus.
- Accreditation and partner claims are what the page says. None were checked with the issuing body.
- Page content changes. This is one reading on 2026-11-11.
