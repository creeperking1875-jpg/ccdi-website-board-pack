# Where we are heading: direction and setups

Start here if you are an AI assistant or a person picking up context. This is the map of what we are building across the website, the digital footprint and GoHighLevel (GHL), how the setups connect, where each one stands, and what happens next. Written 12 November 2026, Philippine time.

## The one-paragraph version

We are building one connected journey: a patient or an HR team finds us online, reaches the website, sends an inquiry, gets a useful approved reply, and ends in a visit or a corporate discussion - with every step recorded and every lead owned by a named person. The website is the front door. The digital footprint is how people find the door. GHL is the back office that records, replies and follows up. We release nothing to customers until its evidence check passes, and we judge every tool by working journeys, not feature counts.

## Where we stand today

- **Live:** the website's homepage and corporate booking entry; search foundations (titles, sitemap, llms.txt, structured data); the updated Google Business Profile description.
- **Prepared, not launched:** the services directory, the price-request form and approved-list email, the customer knowledge base, the staff-reviewed chat assistant, richer corporate intake.
- **Unproven:** contacts saving reliably, confirmation and price-list emails arriving, Facebook message routing, chat handoff to staff, newsletter consent and opt-out.
- **Not started:** the review-request habit, bulk follow-up or newsletters.

## What happens next

1. Confirm the approved facts and name the owners (build lead, lead-response owner and backup, clinical/content approver, privacy/marketing owner).
2. Make one inquiry journey work end to end: the price-request flow, from form to delivered approved list to recorded contact.
3. Test the chat assistant in English, Cebuano/Visaya and mixed language, prove the human handoff, then connect it live.
4. Add the two-path corporate intake (book a slot or request a callback) and verify calendars and emails.
5. Verify Facebook routing, then build the review habit and expand service pages, FAQs and search content.

What justifies continuing GoHighLevel is our own outcomes and actions: inquiries saved, replies delivered, follow-ups owned and recorded. We confirm the real billing date in the account and review that value before renewal. Alternatives are considered only after everything is mapped and set up, and then as a way to lower costs, not to start over.

## Rules that apply everywhere

- No public prices. Price is given on contact through the approved-list request.
- Publish only clinic-confirmed facts. No invented claims, no diagnosing, no booking promises from the assistant.
- Internal records, patient data, client names and rates never enter public content or the customer-facing knowledge base.
- An AI label is not authority: writing assistance first, automation only with explicit triggers and consent, no autonomous sends without review.
- We claim no sales or conversion uplift until the data shows it.
