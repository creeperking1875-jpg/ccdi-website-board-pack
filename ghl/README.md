# CCDI + GoHighLevel: plan and alternatives

This folder turns our GoHighLevel board/family discussion deck into documents that people and AIs can work from. It is the written version of the deck, in our own "we" voice: what we have, what we have not proven, how we will test, and what we still need to decide.

Nothing here is a purchase, activation or cancellation decision. No prices, no client names, no patient data.

## The one-line version

We turn every inquiry - website, chat, Facebook or call - into a recorded lead, a useful approved reply and a named staff follow-up, and we judge GoHighLevel against alternatives on practical tests, not feature counts.

Target journey: website or message → inquiry record + consent → approved reply → assigned staff follow-up → recorded outcome.

## The documents

- [target-workflow.md](target-workflow.md) - the five-step inquiry journey we are building toward, and our ground rules for what "AI marketing" is allowed to mean at CCDI.
- [where-we-stand.md](where-we-stand.md) - what exists today, what is proven, and what is still unproven for each part of the current GHL setup.
- [platform-comparison.md](platform-comparison.md) - GHL vs HubSpot, Zoho, Brevo and Calendly on the same practical tests, plus what two other Philippine clinic sites make visible.
- [staged-pilot.md](staged-pilot.md) - the four release stages and the evidence gate each one must pass before customers touch it.
- [decisions-and-owners.md](decisions-and-owners.md) - the owners we name before stage 1, the decisions we owe each other, and the keep/reduce/replace review.
- [sources.md](sources.md) - every public source behind the comparison, checked 12 November 2026, Philippine time.

## How to read the evidence

This is a plan based on our planning discussions (29 Oct - 11 Nov 2026) and public-page checks on 12 Nov 2026. It is not a fresh audit of our GHL account. No form, booking or customer message was submitted while preparing it, and we claim no conversion or sales improvement.
