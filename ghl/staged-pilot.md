# The staged pilot

Four stages, each behind an evidence gate. We release only when the check passes. Nothing goes to customers on faith.

## Stage 1 - Assistant + records

- Approve customer-facing facts and revised instructions.
- Review existing knowledge for conflicts; add a named human handoff.
- Test English, Cebuano/Visaya and mixed-language questions in staff-review mode.
- **Gate:** a test inquiry creates the right contact and reaches its owner.

## Stage 2 - Price requests + email

- Approve a dated customer list.
- Prove contact saving, request consent, email delivery and staff notification end to end.
- Send the list without requiring newsletter opt-in.
- **Gate:** opt-in, opt-out and duplicate requests tested before any newsletter.

## Stage 3 - Corporate + Facebook

- Add both requested routes: book a call, or leave details and a preferred callback period.
- Confirm calendar availability and emails.
- Check the correct Page and business permissions.
- **Gate:** message routing and staff access tested before connecting customers.

## Stage 4 - Controlled expansion

- Social-media AI for drafts only, with human approval. No automatic medical claims or posting.
- Test only necessary app connections, with limited permissions.
- **Gate:** a connected tool is not proof the customer bot reads updated, approved knowledge - verify it.
