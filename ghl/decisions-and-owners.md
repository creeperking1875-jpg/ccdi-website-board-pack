# Decisions and owners

## Owners we name before stage 1 starts

- **Build lead (proposed: Matthew)** - keeps configuration, test evidence and changes in one checklist.
- **Lead-response owner + backup** - appointed by clinic management. Owns the response target and the follow-up process.
- **Clinical / content approver** - confirms service availability, preparation, hours, turnaround and regulated claims. Our assistant never diagnoses, invents rates, promises bookings or treats independent doctors as clinic employees.
- **Privacy / marketing owner** - approves consent wording, notices, retention and access. We keep request follow-up separate from newsletter opt-in and provide an unsubscribe route.

Boundaries: clinical records and test results stay outside this marketing workflow. This is not a compliance certification.

## Decisions we owe each other

1. **Approve the staged pilot** - assistant and records first; price list, corporate intake and Facebook behind evidence gates.
2. **Name the owners** - lead-response owner and backup, clinical/content approver, privacy/marketing owner.
3. **Confirm the renewal review date** - from the actual account billing date, with the review scheduled before it.
4. **Agree the evidence bar** - what recorded results justify keeping, reducing or replacing the platform.

This plan authorizes no activation, purchase, cancellation or public campaign.

## Keep-or-exit review

We confirm the next billing date in the account and set this review before it. Unlimited Conversation AI was reported; we recheck the actual allowance, fair-use terms and any other enabled usage charges rather than assuming every feature is included.

We judge value on recorded outcomes: saved inquiries, successful deliveries, response time, handoffs, recorded outcomes and staff time saved - against a baseline and review period we set together.

- **Keep** - the tested workflow is useful and owned: inquiries saved, replies delivered, handoffs working.
- **Reduce** - only parts earn their keep: we narrow to what is proven and used, and cut the rest.
- **Replace** - we exit only after checking exports, site/domain migration, forms, contacts, consent history and continuity.

We do not cancel or migrate on the strength of this plan alone. Moving platforms does not fix an unowned process - the same inquiry-to-outcome test decides either way.
