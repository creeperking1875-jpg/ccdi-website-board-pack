## Observed on the page

- The homepage is consumer-facing: "$29 New Patient Exam + X-rays" (age 21 or older per the fine print), same-day emergency care, tooth replacement, "Most Insurance Accepted" with a note that Medicaid is not accepted, the Aspen Dental Savings Plan, an app and a clear-aligner offer.
- Calls to action: "Book Online", "Call to Book", "Contact us". 11 phone links, 7 inputs. Dollar amounts appear on the page.
- No employer or group-benefits page was found. The partnerships page (https://www.aspendental.com/about/partnerships/) lists financing and product partners (American First Finance, Afterpay, CareCredit, Clerri for the savings plan, Dentsply Sirona), not employers. It measured 1,012 words and 28 inputs.
- A web search for an employer or group plan surfaced only the careers benefits page for Aspen staff.

## July 2 claims against the live page

- July 2 node: a corporate hero "Dental benefits your employees will actually use" and "Request a group quote" with a 3-field form. I could not find either on aspendental.com. Treat that July item as not verified.

## Caveats

- Not a like-for-like competitor of CCDI. It is a consumer dental chain with no employer page located.
- Accreditation and partner claims are what the page says. None were checked with the issuing body.
- Page content changes. This is one reading on 2026-11-11.
