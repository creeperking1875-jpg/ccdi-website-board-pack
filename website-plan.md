# The website plan

One page for the whole website programme: why it exists, how it got here, what is live, what is still unproven, and what happens next. Written 12 November 2026, Philippine time. For the reasoning behind each choice, read [CCDI-website-decisions.md](CCDI-website-decisions.md). For the platform side, read the [ghl/](ghl/) folder.

## Why the website exists

The site is not a brochure. It connects four steps into one journey:

**being found online → an inquiry → a useful staff response → a visit or a corporate discussion.**

- Walk-in patients should find the clinic and understand the next step.
- Companies should be able to explain what they need before a call.
- Staff should answer repeated questions less, without replacing clinical judgment.

## Where it stands

**Live and checked:**
- The homepage and the corporate booking entry render publicly.
- Search titles, descriptions, sitemap, robots.txt, llms.txt and structured clinic data are published.
- The Google Business Profile description is updated.

**Prepared, not operational:**
- The services directory design (catalog entries are not proof of current test availability).
- The price-request form and the customer price-list draft. Delivery stays off until the list is approved.
- The customer knowledge base and the staff-reviewed chat assistant.

**Still needs proof:**
- Inquiries reliably save as contacts.
- Confirmation and price-list emails actually arrive.
- Staff can follow up on every lead, with a named owner.
- Chat answers are safe, useful and hand off to a person when needed.
- Facebook message routing works.

## The four journeys the next launch must prove

Each route needs a customer-facing result and a staff-facing result. These are acceptance tests, not claims that they have passed.

1. **Walk-in patient** - finds us in search or Maps, sees confirmed services, hours and contact details, and can call, message or visit without being forced into a corporate form. Pass: a patient can say what to do next, on a phone, and staff give the same answer.
2. **Corporate inquiry** - the employer explains headcount, service needs and timing, then books a slot or leaves a callback request. Pass: the request is saved, the calendar or callback is correct, and no lead is left without an owner.
3. **Price-list request** - the visitor leaves contact details with clear inquiry consent; the newsletter choice stays optional. Pass: the correct, dated, approved list arrives, the contact and consent are recorded, and opt-out works. No corporate-specific rates ever reach the general list.
4. **Chat assistant** - answers English, Cebuano/Visaya and mixed questions from approved facts only. Pass: no invented prices, claims or booking promises, and clinical or uncertain questions reach a person.

## The order we work in

1. Confirm the facts and name the owners.
2. Finish one reliable inquiry and price-list flow end to end.
3. Test the chat assistant and connect it to the live widget.
4. Refine copy, publish approved service pages and FAQs, and expand search activity (see [digital-footprint.md](digital-footprint.md)).

We do not switch on mass follow-up or newsletter delivery just because a form looks finished.

## What the next progress update must show

- Completed changes, with evidence.
- Unfinished work, each with a named owner.
- Inquiry and callback volumes; delivered versus failed emails; lead response time.
- Chat questions handed to staff.
- Search impressions and clicks, with a date range.

No sales or conversion uplift is claimed anywhere in this programme until the data shows it.
