## Content, actions and booking paths

The homepage combines service navigation, visual offers, patient testimonials and news. Current navigation includes Corporate Health Solutions and several scheduling/portal routes. It should not be described as phone-only.

The [executive health check-up scheduler](https://www.healthwaymedicalnetwork.com.ph/find-care/executive-health-check-up-scheduler/) explicitly says visitors can choose a date and time, but appointments are confirmed upon availability. It describes facility follow-up and a processing cut-off. Only the entry instructions were inspected, not a completed booking.

The corporate page emphasizes clinic management, claimed scale and programs rather than a long employer inquiry form. Phone/email remain available. The older diagnostic-listing URL produced conflicting fetch/browser results, so its price-list request cannot be treated as a fully verified current path.

**Lesson for CCDI:** name the kind of booking honestly. A requested appointment, a confirmed clinical slot and a corporate discovery call are different products.

## Method and caveats

- Counts are rendered text-layer tokens containing a letter or number, including navigation/footer and default visible state, not total visual words. Words inside images and closed content are excluded.
- Unique-line counts remove exact repeated lines after whitespace normalization. They are a sensitivity check, not a corrected true count.
- Pages were scrolled to activate lazy sections; dynamic content, carousels and consent state can change the count.
- Desktop only. No conversion, mobile or accessibility audit. No personal data entered, inquiry submitted or appointment booked; availability and delivery were not verified.
- Photography was assessed as photographic imagery versus a placeholder, not authenticated as original. Displayed quality, accreditation and client-count claims were not independently checked. No employer/client identities are reproduced.
- See the comparison node for the full shared method and remaining uncertainty.
- The older diagnostic URL had conflicting evidence: a fetch returned services, while the browser showed an error. Its error-page count is excluded. This does not show that Healthway lacks diagnostic services.
