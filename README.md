## Rundown for AI assistants

Use this section as context. For detail or reasoning, read the decision log. Do not invent facts, prices or turnaround times that are not written here.

About CCDI: Consolacion Community Doctors, Inc. is a diagnostic laboratory and medical clinic in Consolacion, Cebu, Philippines. The website serves two audiences: walk-in patients and company HR teams.

Key decisions:
1. **No prices on the website.** Public prices would help competitors and push CCDI into a price fight. CCDI competes on trust, speed, care and legitimacy. Price is given on contact: a request form sends the price list to the person who asks. Corporate and client-specific rates never go in the general list.
2. **Equal doors, split funnel.** Walk-ins and corporate HR get equal weight. Corporate visitors are filtered at the "Book Call" button.
3. **Call-first, low friction.** The goal is the phone ringing, not a booking app. Bookable corporate call slots are deliberately few.
4. **Corporate intake.** Two paths: book a call slot, or leave details and a preferred period so CCDI calls back. Intake asks richer questions so calls are useful. An FAQ covers fasting, what to bring, results timing and HMO.
5. **What public content may say.** In: services, operations, years in operation, services for company clients. Out: anything about money, anything backend or management-internal. Unconfirmed claims stay out. Never imply visiting doctors are CCDI staff or share their schedules.
6. **Language and tone.** Customers often write in Visaya/Cebuano or mixed language. Understand them without forcing English. Be friendly, clear and plain.
7. **Website chat assistant.** Runs in suggestive mode: it drafts replies and staff approve them. It reads a separate customer-safe knowledge source with no patient data. It must not diagnose, invent prices or turnaround times, or promise bookings.
8. **SEO and AI visibility.** Published: homepage title, per-page metadata, sitemap, robots.txt, llms.txt and structured clinic data. One AI catalog file is saved but not yet published.
9. **Photography.** The photo placeholder was a deliberate choice. Real clinic photos are the top backlog item.

Still undecided (do not treat as settled):
- Whether employer names shown in the "Trusted by Cebu Employers" section stay public.
- Whether Book Call availability (currently none open in November) is intended.
- Rate confirmation for the price list, the student package scope, and thyroid test availability.
- How the chat assistant connects to its knowledge source.
- Final wording of the results-time claim and accreditation wording.
- Whether form contacts save correctly and confirmation emails send.
