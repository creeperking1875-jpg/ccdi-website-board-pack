# The digital footprint plan

How people find CCDI before they ever reach the website, and what we do about it. Written 12 November 2026, Philippine time. This covers discovery only: Google, Maps, reviews, Facebook and search foundations. The website itself is in [website-plan.md](website-plan.md); the platform setup is in the [ghl/](ghl/) folder.

## The idea

Most patients and HR teams meet CCDI on a search results page or a map before they see ccdilab.com. So discovery is part of the same programme as the website, not a separate marketing project: **be findable, look consistent, earn reviews, and make the next step obvious.**

## The rules

- **Consistent details everywhere.** Name, address, hours and phone must match across the website, the Google listing and Facebook. Inconsistency costs trust and rankings.
- **Reviews over polish.** A steady flow of honest reviews matters more than more site refinement right now.
- **No price competition in public.** Discovery copy sells trust, speed, care and legitimacy. Prices stay on request, per decision 1 in [CCDI-website-decisions.md](CCDI-website-decisions.md).
- **Only confirmed facts.** No accreditation, availability or results-timing claims until the clinic confirms them. Real photos replace the placeholder when we have them.

## What we measure

Search impressions and clicks (always with a date range), Maps visibility, review count and quality over time, and how many inquiries say they found us through Google or Facebook. Earlier speed and ranking snapshots are historical, not current guarantees.
