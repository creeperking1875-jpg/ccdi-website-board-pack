## Observed on the page

- The measured page is a group overview, not an employer or occupational-health page. It says the Malaysian arm began operations in late 1997 and is headquartered in Petaling Jaya, Selangor, and lists GP clinics, dental clinics and medical imaging centres.
- Page structure: an overview block, Vision / Mission / Values, then Primary Care and Dental Care service lists. Services are listed by service name, not by employer benefit.
- The services text includes "FOMEMA and EMGS (foreign students check-up)".
- The Mission line reads "To be The preferred healthcare provider delivering quality care to the community."
- Only call to action found: "Contact Us". No phone link, no peso/ringgit amounts, no booking widget. 2 forms with 5 inputs in total (not opened).

## July 2 claims against the live page

- July 2 node: "1,000+ companies", "ISO", services structured by employer benefit. None of these appeared in the text of this page (no "1,000" or "ISO" match). They may sit on other pages that were not measured.
- July 2 node: generic "preferred provider" tagline. The phrase appears in the Mission statement on this page.

## Caveats

- Only one page was measured. An employer-services or occupational-health page on the Qualitas site, if one exists, was not located.
- Accreditation and partner claims are what the page says. None were checked with the issuing body.
- Page content changes. This is one reading on 2026-11-11.
