## Observed on the page

- This is the Society of Occupational Medicine's free, open directory of UK and international occupational-health companies. It is a directory, so it says nothing about how a single provider presents itself.
- Entries seen near the top: Medigold Health, Wrightway Health, KML Occupational Health, Occua Ltd, Insight Workplace Health, Ad Hoc Occupational Health Limited, Global Occupational Health Solutions.
- The listings mention quality certifications including SEQOHS, ISO 9001, ISO 14001, ISO 27001, ISO 45001, RISQS, Cyber Essentials and CQC.
- Disclaimer on the page: the SOM does not assure the competence or expertise of listed companies, and employers must check suitability, for example whether a company is SEQOHS accredited.
- No amounts on the page. 1 phone link, 2 forms with 7 inputs (not opened).

## July 2 claims against the live page

- July 2 node: OH Assist reached via the SOM directory, small UK firms showing "from £X" prices, SEQOHS above the fold. The directory page shows no amounts and I did not confirm that OH Assist is listed on it. No first-party OH Assist site was identified (see the not-identified node).

## Caveats

- The long page (1,239 words, 235 lines) is list content. Its counts should not be compared with a single provider's homepage.
- Accreditation and partner claims are what the page says. None were checked with the issuing body.
- Page content changes. This is one reading on 2026-11-11.
