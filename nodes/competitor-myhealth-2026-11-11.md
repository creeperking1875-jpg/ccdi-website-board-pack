## Content, actions and booking paths

The homepage uses a brief hero and "Call Now," then mostly service titles, a featured-package teaser, partner imagery, a locator and trust/tenure statements. The laboratory page explains the service in two paragraphs. The employer/on-site page is short, lists annual checkups, vaccination and phlebotomy, and directs inquiries to a sales email.

The products listing is more descriptive than the homepage and gives package summaries. No numeric price was present in the captured listing; individual detail screens were not audited. The branch locator includes Cebu. Old claims about broken corporate pages or schedulers should not be carried over to this current site without a fresh test.

**Lesson for CCDI:** move depth to the destination page while letting the homepage act as a clear menu. This is the strongest low-copy comparator in the sample that does not depend entirely on image lettering.

## Method and caveats

- Counts are rendered text-layer tokens containing a letter or number, including navigation/footer and default visible state, not total visual words. Words inside images and closed content are excluded.
- Unique-line counts remove exact repeated lines after whitespace normalization. They are a sensitivity check, not a corrected true count.
- Pages were scrolled to activate lazy sections; dynamic content, carousels and consent state can change the count.
- Desktop only. No conversion, mobile or accessibility audit. No personal data entered, inquiry submitted or appointment booked; availability and delivery were not verified.
- Photography was assessed as photographic imagery versus a placeholder, not authenticated as original. Displayed quality, accreditation and client-count claims were not independently checked. No employer/client identities are reproduced.
- See the comparison node for the full shared method and remaining uncertainty.
