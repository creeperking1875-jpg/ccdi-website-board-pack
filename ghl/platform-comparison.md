# Platform comparison

We compare the current GHL build with alternatives using the same practical tests, not feature counts. These are published product capabilities, not tested CCDI configurations. Feature access, add-ons and usage allowances must be confirmed on our own account. No price comparison is included. Numbered references are in [sources.md](sources.md).

This comparison is a shortlist, not an exhaustive market review or a purchase recommendation.

## Calendars and corporate intake

A booking is only one step. Corporate discovery calls are not employee examination bookings: we collect the company's need and timing first, and we keep a callback-request route for teams not ready to choose a slot.

| Option | Published scheduling approach | Our implication / open check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| GHL [3-4] | Native calendar custom forms can map answers into CRM; the form is completed after selecting a time. Notifications cover booking states, reminders, rescheduling and follow-up. | Can support richer intake, but our custom site must be connected and tested. A native capability does not prove the current form mapping or email delivery. |
| HubSpot [5, 8] | Calendar-connected booking links, round-robin/group meetings and CRM-linked scheduling; Customer Agent can help qualify and book. | A suite candidate for intake plus follow-up. Confirm the chosen edition and demonstrate required custom intake and callback handling. |
| Zoho [9, 12] | Bookings offers multiple meeting types, calendar sync and scheduling rules. CRM integration supports lead/contact updates and meeting information. | A connected-suite candidate. Check app-to-app mappings, permissions and duplicate handling, not just the booking page. |
| Brevo [13] | Conversations includes Meetings alongside chat and inbox tools. | A plausible marketing/chat/meeting setup. Complex corporate routing and a comparable autonomous bot were not verified in the reviewed pages. |
| Calendly [16] | Routing forms ask questions before directing visitors to an event, custom message or external page. CRM ownership routing is advertised. | A scheduling specialist, not a complete replacement for the reviewed marketing/chat stack. We would connect chosen CRM/email/chat tools and test consent and data sync. |

Design difference worth knowing: GHL's form comes after slot selection; Calendly qualifies before routing. A distinction, not a reason to switch by itself.

## What other clinics make visible

- **Healthway** [17]: its executive-checkup scheduler lets visitors choose a date/time, but confirmation depends on availability, and facility updates come by email or phone. It also asks whether a company representative is making the request. A useful model for honest request-versus-confirmation wording.
- **Clinica Prime** [18-19]: a corporate-services page invites a tailored-services inquiry through a form; its FAQ directs appointment requests to the official Facebook Page for customer-care help. A staff-assisted path, not proof of a self-serve final slot or an AI workflow.

Not observable from public pages: these clinics' underlying CRM, chatbot provider, newsletter automation, deliverability or conversion results. We submitted no forms or bookings. Public pages do not establish that either clinic uses GHL or any alternative above.
