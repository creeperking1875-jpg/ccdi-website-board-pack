## Method (same for every page)

- Real desktop Chrome, signed out, page loaded plus about four seconds, viewport about 993 px tall. Nothing was submitted, booked or logged in. Forms were counted, not opened.
- Words = whitespace-separated tokens of the rendered visible text (innerText). This includes cookie banners and carousel repeats and excludes words inside images.
- Header/nav/footer = text inside top-level header, nav and footer elements. First screen = text whose top edge falls inside the first viewport.
- Lines and distinct lines show how much text repeats. Keyword checks for amounts and accreditation names are literal text matches only. They miss anything that is an image.
- The earlier same-day competitor report was not available to this task, so this method was built to match its description (rendered text counts, nav and footer split, duplicate sensitivity). Counts may differ in small ways from that report. Compare with care.

## What the live pages showed against the July 2 text

- **Figures differ.** Concentra now shows 630+ centers and 12,000+ colleagues (July: 520 and 11,000). Workplace Options shows 150K+ organizations (July: 50,000+).
- **Not found in page text.** Qualitas "1,000+ companies"; Halodoc's 3-field demo form and badge logos; BookDoc's named enterprise clients; DoctorOnCall's Pfizer and named clients; Aspen Dental's group-quote page; Optima and Fusion accreditation strips. For logos or images this means unverified, not absent.
- **Different call to action.** BookDoc shows "Request Demo", not "Get a Quote". Fusion shows generic Contact buttons, not a named adviser.
- **Amounts.** No amounts on any employer or corporate page measured. Consumer pages of Halodoc, DoctorOnCall and Aspen Dental do show amounts. The SOM directory and provider-level UK package prices ("from £X") were not seen.
- **Page type.** Qualitas, Concentra and Aspen Dental resolved to a group overview or consumer homepage, not an employer page. Several pages sell something different from lab and clinic work: BookDoc (wellness app), Workplace Options (EAP counselling), Halodoc and DoctorOnCall (platforms).

## Limits

- One or two pages per site, one day. Sites not clearly comparable to a local lab are noted in each node.
- Accreditation and client claims are as stated on the pages and were not verified.
- No traffic, ranking or conversion data. The July note's idea of pulling traffic figures was not done here.
