## Content, actions and booking paths

The hospital/network homepage uses a photographic campaign hero, doctor/service/HMO actions, locations, institutional proof and news. The services page is an extensive hospital taxonomy. Its JCI-related proof is a claim displayed by the site, not independently verified in this audit. A booking action is visible in the header; its completion path and corporate-specific equivalent were not audited.

**Lesson for CCDI:** clear service categories and proof placement are useful references. A hospital's content breadth and institutional claims are not appropriate targets for a small laboratory's word count or positioning.

## Method and caveats

- Counts are rendered text-layer tokens containing a letter or number, including navigation/footer and default visible state, not total visual words. Words inside images and closed content are excluded.
- Unique-line counts remove exact repeated lines after whitespace normalization. They are a sensitivity check, not a corrected true count.
- Pages were scrolled to activate lazy sections; dynamic content, carousels and consent state can change the count.
- Desktop only. No conversion, mobile or accessibility audit. No personal data entered, inquiry submitted or appointment booked; availability and delivery were not verified.
- Photography was assessed as photographic imagery versus a placeholder, not authenticated as original. Displayed quality, accreditation and client-count claims were not independently checked. No employer/client identities are reproduced.
