## Observed on the page

- The page is in Indonesian. My English glosses below are my own translation.
- Services listed: cashless and reimbursement support for insurance use, on-site employee medical check-ups, on-site vaccination, vitamin C injections, online doctor-consultation packages, and health tips.
- A block reads "Min. Pesan untuk 25 Karyawan" (minimum order for 25 employees). Another says homecare is 5x more affordable than other lab testing, and that results are delivered in the app.
- A partners block says "1000+ Perusahaan dan 20+ Penyedia Asuransi" (1000+ companies and 20+ insurance providers).
- No form or input fields on this page, and no amounts in the page text. The consumer homepage (https://www.halodoc.com/) shows rupiah prices for products (for example Rp24.900); it measured 548 words.

## July 2 claims against the live page

- July 2 node: demo request with 3 fields (name, company, employee count); Ministry of Health badge; JCI partner hospitals; Tokopedia, Gojek and Grab logos. None of these appeared on the corporate page text, and it has no form. The 25-employee minimum is a different way of showing employee count.

## Caveats

- The July source may have meant a different Halodoc enterprise page. Only /corporate-partnership was measured. Words in Indonesian are counted the same way as English.
- Accreditation and partner claims are what the page says. None were checked with the issuing body.
- Page content changes. This is one reading on 2026-11-11.
