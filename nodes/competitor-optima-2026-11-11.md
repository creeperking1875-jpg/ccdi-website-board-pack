## Observed on the page

- H1: "We optimise workplace health and wellbeing". Top banner: "Digital Flu Vouchers available now".
- Positioning text: "a leading occupational health and wellbeing company in the UK and Ireland", with blocks titled Smarter health services, Easy access, Digital experience and Successful outcomes.
- A notice says the PAM brand is changing to Optima Health after PAM Group was acquired by Optima Health.
- The navigation has an "Accreditations" link. No accreditation names or numbers appeared in the homepage text itself.
- One call to action: "Contact". No forms, no phone link, no amounts. Two client quotes appear as text.

## July 2 claims against the live page

- July 2 node: SEQOHS + ISO 9001/27001/14001 strip, RISQS and Cyber Essentials. These were not visible in the homepage text. They may be images or sit on the Accreditations page, which was not opened.
