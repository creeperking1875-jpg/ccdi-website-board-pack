## Observed on the page

- Page text: "A Wellness Programme That Works!", about a lifestyle-management programme with digital tools, private virtual challenges, rewards and gamification, and early risk detection.
- Plans named: BookDoc Premium and BookDoc Premium+. Calls to action: "Request Demo", "Contact Us", "Search and Book", and a health talks item. A section titled "Our Partners and Clients" is present.
- The page title says "HRDC Claimable". The page text was not read for the scheme's terms.
- No amounts. 2 inputs, 3 phone/tel links, 91 images. Company names were not found in the page text.

## July 2 claims against the live page

- July 2 node: named enterprise clients (Petronas, Shell, Maybank) as logos above the fold; "Get a Quote" leading to an account manager. The company names did not appear as text, so if they are logos they are unverified. The live call to action is "Request Demo", not "Get a Quote".

## Caveats

- This page sells a wellness programme and app, not clinic screening. A different BookDoc corporate-screening page may exist; one blog post about free corporate screening surfaced in search and was not opened.
- Accreditation and partner claims are what the page says. None were checked with the issuing body.
- Page content changes. This is one reading on 2026-11-11.
