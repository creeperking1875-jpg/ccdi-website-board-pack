## Observed on the page

- H1: "Keep Your Workforce Healthy, Safe and Productive." Sub-text covers nationwide occupational health, health surveillance and wellbeing programmes for UK organisations, SMEs to large enterprises.
- Claims on the page: "Trusted by over 3,800 businesses"; the largest mobile fleet in the UK; the only occupational-health provider offering a mobile chest X-ray service; "the world's first Connected Health Platform" (YODHA).
- Calls to action: "Contact Us", "Get a quote", "Book Now", "Call". 5 phone links, 1 form with 6 inputs (not opened). No amounts.
- Header, nav and footer account for 311 of 759 words (a large menu), the highest chrome share of the 11 pages. A cookie banner is included in the count.
- Sections: webinars, "The Vault" client hub, About Us.

## July 2 claims against the live page

- July 2 node: educational positioning ("what OH is and why it matters") plus SEQOHS. The homepage is service and platform led. No SEQOHS text matched. An explainer page, if one exists, was not measured. The word "accredited" appears for training services only.
