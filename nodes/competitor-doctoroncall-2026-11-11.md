## Observed on the page

- Corporate screening page (https://www.doctoroncall.com.my/corporate/screening): 219 words. It says screening is on-site and at partner labs nationwide, positions it as cost-effective, and says a dedicated team coordinates appointments and reports. Calls to action: "Book a FREE Demo" and "Get Enquiry". An interest form has 9 inputs, including "Where is your company located?" (not submitted).
- Registration page (https://www.doctoroncall.com.my/corporate/registration), titled "DoctorOnCall Corporate | Request Demo Account": 193 words. It says "Malaysia's largest healthcare platform, with 15 million user base" and "Partner of multinational companies, government bodies and a network of hospitals & pharmacies", without naming any. 5 inputs; button "Send Request" (not submitted).
- The consumer homepage (https://www.doctoroncall.com.my/) measured 1,492 words and shows ringgit prices for consultations ("start from just RM12.99").
- No amounts on the corporate pages. Also a WhatsApp number in the footer text.

## July 2 claims against the live page

- July 2 node: Ministry of Health authorization, Pfizer partnership, named enterprise clients. None appeared in the text of the two corporate pages. A corporate-partners page at doctoroncall.com surfaced in search and was not opened.

## Caveats

- The corporate pages are very short (under 220 words each) next to the 1,492-word homepage. The script found no header or footer elements on these two pages, so chrome is recorded as 0.
- Accreditation and partner claims are what the page says. None were checked with the issuing body.
- Page content changes. This is one reading on 2026-11-11.
